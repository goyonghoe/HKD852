## Usage Tips for AI Image Generation

### General Best Practices:

- **Replace `[product]` placeholders** with your specific product name
- **Adjust aspect ratios** based on your platform (1:1 for Instagram, 4:5 for Pinterest, 16:9 for websites)
- **Add `--style raw`** for more photographic, less artistic results
- **Specify your product material** (leather, metal, glass) for better texture rendering
- **Include brand names** in prompts for style consistency (e.g., "Apple-style minimalism")

### Platform-Specific Adjustments:

**For Midjourney:**

- Add `--v 6` or latest version number at the end
- Use `--q 2` for maximum quality
- Add `--no text, watermark` to avoid unwanted elements

**For DALL-E 3:**

- Remove `--ar` parameters (DALL-E uses different syntax)
- Add "photorealistic, professional photography" for best results
- Specify "no text on image" if needed

**For Stable Diffusion:**

- Add negative prompts: "blurry, low quality, distorted, amateur"
- Increase steps to 50+ for product photography
- Use realistic checkpoints like "Realistic Vision" or "Dreamshaper"

### Cost Optimization:

- Start with lower resolution for testing
- Batch similar products with same prompt style
- Use seed numbers to maintain consistency across product lines
