How to Use This Prompt Pack

Quick Start (2 Minutes)

  1. Pick a category that matches your product (e.g., "Jewelry & Accessories")
  2. Copy any prompt from that category
  3. Replace [product] or [YOUR PRODUCT] with your actual product description
  4. Paste into your AI tool (Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion)
  5. Generate and download your product photo

That's it. No photography skills needed.

Customization Guide

Replacing the Product Placeholder

Bad: [product] → "my item" Good: [product] → "handmade silver ring with blue topaz gemstone, thin band"

The more specific you are, the better the result. Include: - Material (leather, ceramic, stainless steel) - Color (matte black, rose gold, forest green) - Size/Shape (compact, oversized, rectangular) - Key Feature (textured surface, transparent lid, embossed logo)

Adjusting Aspect Ratios

Platform Recommended Ratio Use
Instagram Feed --ar 1:1 Square product posts
Instagram Story/Reels --ar 9:16 Vertical stories
Pinterest --ar 2:3 or --ar 4:5 Tall pins
Amazon/Shopify --ar 1:1 Product listings
Website Hero --ar 16:9 Banner images
Etsy Listing --ar 4:3 Landscape thumbnails

Modifying Lighting

Add these modifiers to any prompt: - Brighter: add "high key lighting, bright and airy" - Moodier: add "low key lighting, dramatic shadows" - Warmer: add "warm golden light, sunset tones" - Cooler: add "cool blue tones, daylight balanced" - Studio: add "professional studio strobe lighting, softbox"

Changing Backgrounds

Replace background descriptions with: - pure white background — Clean marketplace standard - light grey seamless background — Professional catalog - black background — Premium/luxury feel - natural lifestyle setting — Contextual marketing - wooden table surface — Rustic/artisanal - marble surface — High-end aesthetic

Platform-Specific Tips

Midjourney

[prompt text] --ar 1:1 --v 6 --style raw --q 2
  • --v 6 — Use latest model version
  • --style raw — More photographic, less artistic
  • --q 2 — Maximum quality
  • --no text, watermark, logo — Remove unwanted elements
  • --s 100 — Lower stylization for realism

DALL-E 3 (ChatGPT)

  • Remove all --ar, --style raw parameters
  • Add "photorealistic, professional product photography" at the end
  • Specify "no text overlays on the image"
  • Request specific dimensions: "Generate as a 1024x1024 image"

Stable Diffusion
