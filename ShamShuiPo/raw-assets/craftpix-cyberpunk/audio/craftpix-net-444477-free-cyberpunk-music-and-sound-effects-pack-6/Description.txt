Pack #6 includes

5 Music Tracks:

Main_theme_Chinese_Street – main theme for the Chinese Street location
Alternative_theme_Chinese_Street – alternative version of the theme for the Chinese location
Calm_theme_Chinese_Street – calm melody, suitable for a main menu or any relaxed, non-intense scene
Battle_theme_Chinese_Street – battle theme for the Chinese location
Stealthy_theme_loopable – loopable theme for tense stealth moments where the player must avoid enemies and stay hidden


Sounds inspired by the Chinese Street series packs:

Unlocked_chest – chest opening sound

Laser_sword_1 / Laser_sword_2 – samurai laser sword sounds
Samurai_death – samurai death sound, falling armor effect
Samurai_footstep_1 / Samurai_footstep_2 – two variations of samurai footsteps with rattling armor

Explosion – explosion of a wheeled barrel
Karateka_attack – karate fighter strike

Hovering_robot_sting – stinger attack sound of a hovering spine-like robot
Hovering_robot_walk_loopable – loopable movement sound of the hovering spine-like robot

Flying_platform_attack_1 / Flying_platform_attack_2 – two attack variations of the flying platform carrying a geisha
Bomb_drop – sound of the boss tractor dropping ammunition


Sounds inspired by the Doors and Portals pack:

Sliding_doors – sliding door sound
Roller_doors – roller door sound
Creaking_wooden_door – creaking wooden door sound
Door_with_password – locked door secured with a password
Push_slide_door – sliding compartment-style door that is pushed forward and slides open

Zipline_loopable – loopable zipline sliding sound

Lift_mechanism – lift mechanism sound
Elevator_motor – elevator motor sound

Portal_1 / Portal_2 – two variations of portal opening sounds
Portal_moving – portal travel sound

Sounds inspired by the Art Market Location pack:

Bell_on_the_door – door bell chimes, also known as wind chimes; can be used before opening a shop inventory

Melody_of_attraction_loopable – loopable melody to attract the player's attention to a vending machine or interactive object
